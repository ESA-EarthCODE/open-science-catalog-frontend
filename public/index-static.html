Welcome to the Open Science Catalogue

A catalogue of geoscience products, datasets, software, workflows, and research resources from ESA and partner initiatives, FAIR-enhanced through EarthCODE services and openly shared in accordance with ESA Open Science principles.

Contribute

To suggest changes and/or contribute to the continuously growing number of available products, you can do so directly via the STAC catalog hosted on GitHub, or use the tools and services available in EarthCODE workspace.
